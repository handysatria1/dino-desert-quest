FOR YOUR GROWN-UP

More play. Less pressure.

A short, forgiving adventure for an early-primary child. There are no enemies, timers, lost lives, ads, music, or purchases. Falling in water returns the explorer safely to the bank.

Three places, always available

The dinosaur valley and Egypt are separate, imaginary museum-inspired worlds, not the same historical period. Completing their exploration missions earns keepsake stamps. The quiet camp is always open: prayer and Qur’an practice never earn points or gate the adventure.

Islamic learning

Helping, gratitude, and caring for creation are woven into the story. The camp opens the existing Muhammadiyah-style prayer guide and Al-Fatihah plus the last three surahs. English computer voices read English guidance only. Qur’an uses recorded Al-Husary recitation (internet needed); prayer duas remain parent-led. Please review prayer content with your teacher. No depictions of Allah or prophets.

Controls & accessibility

Arrow keys or A/D to move, Space/W/↑ to jump, E/Enter to explore, Escape to pause. Hold the touch arrows; tap Jump and Explore. Tap a place in the scenery to walk there. Puzzles support selecting a piece then a slot, keyboard buttons, or dragging. In the map, “Activity list” offers direct, movement-free access.

Saved on this browser only

Only completed exploration stamps, outfit colour, and voice preference are saved. No name, birthday, microphone, analytics, or religious-performance data. Browser voices vary. Visuals and gameplay work offline.
