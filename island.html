 A SMALL ISLAND. A BIG IMAGINATION.

For your grown-up

An active 3D adventure for ages 5–6, with no enemies, countdowns, lost lives, music, ads, accounts, or purchases. The original illustrated game is still available.

Let him lead

Gather leaves by walking into the floating snacks above leafy bushes. Share a leaf with a roaming dinosaur to make a friend and ride. At a dig, rub the sand away with a finger or mouse; a brush button also works by keyboard. Collect eggs, bring them to the nursery, and tap the egg button four times to watch a baby hatch. The baby can follow him, or return to its nest with the baby button.

Play, not just collect

Tap Hop trail for a repeatable six-hoop adventure: walk or ride through the golden hoops and hop inside the high ones. There is no timer or failure penalty. Pip can lead the way, but high hoops still need a hop. The beach ball rolls when pushed; try chasing it on a dinosaur. All play is optional. Dinosaur snacks regrow; there are no timed care chores.

Tap the ground, use the joystick, or use arrow keys/WASD; E explores, Space hops. Tap Pip for help. The map can guide him to any place. The helping-hand button changes with nearby objects. Dinosaurs wait when approached, so he never has to chase one to interact.

Quiet learning, always optional

The quiet camp has the existing parent-guided Muhammadiyah prayer sequence and Al-Fatihah, Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, and An-Nas. Learning never awards points or unlocks exploration. Please review religious material with a qualified teacher; references are in the learning guide. No depictions of Allah or prophets.

Pretend discoveries

Stylised dinosaurs, simplified fossils, and fantasy eggs are for play, not scientific reconstructions. The decorative pyramid is a separate museum model, not a claim that people in ancient Egypt lived with dinosaurs.

Saved here only

Discovered dinosaurs, eggs, fossils, displays, hatched babies, leaves, companion choice, hop-trail keepsake, explorer position, and voice preference are stored on this browser. Partial brushing, unfinished hatching, moving dinosaur locations, and ball position are not saved across refreshes. No personal information, microphones, or religious-performance records. Recitation is streamed only after a tap; third-party audio hosts receive ordinary network requests. English speech uses your device’s available voice.

Three.js r160.1 · MIT licence. All models are made from local procedural shapes. An internet connection is not needed for the island’s artwork or gameplay.
