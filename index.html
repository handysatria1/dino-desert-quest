A little guidance.

This is a parent-guided learning prototype, not a prayer assessment or a substitute for a teacher. Try one phrase or one ayah at a time.

Prayer approach

A two-rak‘ah practice sequence with commonly taught Muhammadiyah wording. Iftitah uses “Allahumma ba‘id…”. Valid variants exist. Please review with your Muhammadiyah teacher before teaching; this prototype is not officially endorsed.

English voice reads instructions only. Prayer duas are parent-led; recorded dua audio is not included. English meanings are labelled summaries, not exact translations. Transliteration is a reading aid, not a tajwid guide.

Qur’an and audio

Bundled Arabic: AlQuran Cloud, quran-simple. English: Saheeh International. Transliteration: en.transliteration. Recorded ayah recitation: Mahmoud Khalil Al-Husary, EveryAyah (128 kbps). Audio needs internet. No synthetic Qur’an recitation, background music, ads, accounts, or microphone.

History stays history

Fossils and ancient Egypt are separate museum-inspired adventures, not events in one time period. Pyramids are historical structures, not places of Islamic worship. Religious text stays in the learning spaces, never buried in the dig.

References & content notes

    The full dua before salam is included. Its literal references to punishment and trials appear only in the grown-up details; the child sees a gentle, explicitly labelled summary.

    Only visited-place stickers and the voice preference are saved on this browser. No recordings or personal details are collected. External audio hosts receive ordinary requests when you press play.
