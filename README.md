# Pip’s Wonder World

## [Play Wonder World](https://handysatria1.github.io/dino-desert-quest/world.html)

A gentle open-world educational park for ages 5–6. Drive a little buggy continuously between seven landmark districts, or use big picture travel cards. A joystick, hop, one changing gold action button, and Pip’s spoken English guidance replace the old crowded play screen. No independent reading required; browser speech availability varies by device.

### Explore the New Seven Wonders

| Place | Hands-on play |
| --- | --- |
| Great Wall · China | Carry three stones and build a pretend model wall. |
| Petra · Jordan | Follow coloured arches in sequence; replays reverse the trail. |
| Taj Mahal · India | Water three matching pairs of flower beds and discover symmetry. |
| Colosseum · Italy | Drag or tap shapes to build a toy arch with two supports and a curved top. |
| Chichén Itzá · Mexico | Count five stepping stones, hopping on the two high ones. |
| Machu Picchu · Peru | Guide a following llama through terrace paths and into its meadow. |
| Corcovado · Brazil | Plant and water three trees, then watch birds return. |

The seventh site is the location of Christ the Redeemer. **No statue or religious figure is depicted**, respecting the family’s no-prophet-depictions requirement; the game represents the mountain, forest and viewpoint instead. All seven destinations are open immediately. Models are stylised miniature replicas in an imaginary park, not geographic neighbours, historical reconstructions, or activities on actual protected monuments.

**Child controls:** slide the movement circle or tap the ground; tap the gold button to ride, plant, water, build or help; tap Pip’s face for a guided route; tap the globe to choose a picture. Arrow keys/WASD, Space and E also work. The buggy has forgiving steering, no fuel, traffic, countdowns, crashes or failure penalties. Every activity can be replayed; collected postcards remain.

**Grown-ups:** pause for audio, always-open quiet camp, voice controls, source/representation notes and optional reset. Existing Muhammadiyah prayer and four-surah learning remain unchanged and never award points, earn postcards or gate exploration. Optional Juz Amma still requires an explicit tap and pauses for narration, activities, learning, menus and hidden tabs. No background music, combat, crime, weapons, enemies, purchases or ads.

Wonder activities, postcards, replay variation, voice setting and position save only in this browser under a separate key; old dinosaur progress is preserved. No names, birthdays, microphone access, accounts, analytics or server-side saves. Static scenery is merged by material for lighter rendering. Local Three.js and all models are bundled; no runtime graphics CDN, build step or generated image downloads.

## Dinosaur island is still here

### [Play the 3D dinosaur island](https://handysatria1.github.io/dino-desert-quest/island.html)

A gentle, active browser adventure for little explorers, designed around ages 5–6.

- Roaming dinosaurs stop to meet you. Gather renewable leaves, share a snack, then ride a dinosaur.
- Explore for hidden eggs. Tap to crack an egg, watch it hatch, and take a baby dinosaur for a walk.
- Rub sand away with a finger or mouse to reveal fossils, then fill your 3D museum. A keyboard-friendly brush button is also available.
- Walk or ride through six golden hoops, hopping through the high ones. No timer or lost lives. Replay whenever you like.
- Push and chase a rolling beach ball. Explore with Pip, a picture map, spoken English guidance, and saved discoveries.

### New: the teamwork expedition

Tap **🧭 Expedition** to play a connected adventure:

1. Invite a lost baby to follow, and walk it home to the nursery.
2. Carry three timbers; ride Lulu to use her long reach for the high one.
3. Fit six planks to build an animated bridge to a second island.
4. Ride Toto and use his strength to roll a boulder away from a bright, open-roof cave.
5. Follow the cave footprints, hop on the middle one, and brush away sand to reveal a giant fossil.

Tap Pip’s face for the next step and a safe walking route. The map covers both islands. Mission progress saves, including a partly built bridge or partly moved boulder. Tap Expedition again to switch back to free exploration; your mission progress stays. The quiet camp is always available and never part of mission requirements.

**Tablet:** slide the joystick, or tap the ground to walk. The gold action button changes when you find something. Tap **Hop trail** for the hoop activity, and Pip’s face for help.

**Computer:** arrows/WASD to move, Space to hop, E to interact, Escape to pause.

The homepage’s Play button opens Wonder World. Its picture menu also leads to Dinosaur Island. The [original illustrated Dino & Desert Quest](https://handysatria1.github.io/dino-desert-quest/play.html) remains available, including its Egypt adventure.

### Quiet learning and optional recitation

The quiet camp is always open: parent-guided Muhammadiyah-style prayer learning and Al-Fatihah, Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, and An-Nas. Religious practice never gives game rewards or unlocks exploration. Review prayer material with a qualified teacher; references and caveats are in the learning guide. No depictions of Allah or prophets.

Tap **Qur’an audio → Start recitation** for optional recorded Juz Amma. The [community Internet Archive upload](https://archive.org/details/MuzammilHasballahJuzAmma) is labelled Muzammil Hasballah; it is not independently authenticated as an official release. No recording is redistributed in this repository. Streaming requires internet and may use substantial mobile data; Wi-Fi is recommended. Playback pauses for narration, activities, lessons, menus, breaks, hidden tabs, and grown-up notes. Individual ayah practice uses the existing Al-Husary recordings from EveryAyah.

### Privacy and compatibility

No accounts, ads, purchases, microphone, analytics, combat, enemies, or religious-performance scores. Discoveries, leaves, baby companion choice, completed hop-trail keepsake, expedition progress, position, and English voice preference stay in this browser’s local storage. No names or birthdays are stored. Third-party audio/font hosts receive ordinary network requests.

Use a recent Safari or Chrome with WebGL. If 3D is unavailable, use the illustrated adventure. Island graphics and scripts are bundled locally; no build step or runtime CDN is needed. No guaranteed offline installation is provided. Browser speech voices vary. Dinosaurs, fossils, and hatching eggs are stylised pretend play; the decorative pyramid is a labelled museum model, not a shared dinosaur/Egypt timeline.

Three.js is pinned to npm **0.160.1** under the included [MIT licence](vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt). The pinned classic build emits an upstream deprecation warning. All game models are procedurally constructed from shapes.
