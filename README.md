# Dino & Desert Quest

A gentle browser adventure for little explorers. Run, jump, help a dinosaur, build a fossil display, repair a bridge, and sail down the Nile. No build step required.

## [Play the game](https://handysatria1.github.io/dino-desert-quest/play.html)

Tablet: hold the arrows, tap Jump and Explore. Computer: arrows/A/D to move, Space to jump, E to explore. The map includes movement-free activities and an always-open prayer/Qur’an camp.

Optional background Juz Amma is streamed from a community Internet Archive upload labelled Muzammil Hasballah: https://archive.org/details/MuzammilHasballahJuzAmma . This is not an independently authenticated official release. No audio files are redistributed here. Press Qur’an audio to start; internet is required. Playback pauses for narration, lessons, pause screens, and hidden tabs.

The prayer guide uses common Muhammadiyah wording and needs parent/qualified-teacher review. Individual-ayah practice uses recorded Al-Husary audio from EveryAyah. Arabic text, Saheeh International meanings, and transliteration are from AlQuran Cloud. See the in-game grown-up notes for references and caveats. No religious-performance scores, prophet depictions, ads, accounts, purchases, or microphone.

Exploration stamps, outfit colour, and English voice preference stay in browser local storage. No names, dates of birth, recordings, or analytics. Third-party audio/font hosts receive ordinary network requests. Fossils and Egypt are separate imaginary museum-inspired settings, not the same historical era.
