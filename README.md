# Pip’s Dinosaur Island

## [Play the 3D island](https://handysatria1.github.io/dino-desert-quest/island.html)

A gentle, active browser adventure for little explorers, designed around ages 5–6.

- Roaming dinosaurs stop to meet you. Gather renewable leaves, share a snack, then ride a dinosaur.
- Explore for hidden eggs. Tap to crack an egg, watch it hatch, and take a baby dinosaur for a walk.
- Rub sand away with a finger or mouse to reveal fossils, then fill your 3D museum. A keyboard-friendly brush button is also available.
- Walk or ride through six golden hoops, hopping through the high ones. No timer or lost lives. Replay whenever you like.
- Push and chase a rolling beach ball. Explore with Pip, a picture map, spoken English guidance, and saved discoveries.

**Tablet:** slide the joystick, or tap the ground to walk. The gold action button changes when you find something. Tap **Hop trail** for the hoop activity, and Pip’s face for help.

**Computer:** arrows/WASD to move, Space to hop, E to interact, Escape to pause.

The homepage’s Play button opens the island. The [original illustrated Dino & Desert Quest](https://handysatria1.github.io/dino-desert-quest/play.html) remains available, including its Egypt adventure.

### Quiet learning and optional recitation

The quiet camp is always open: parent-guided Muhammadiyah-style prayer learning and Al-Fatihah, Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, and An-Nas. Religious practice never gives game rewards or unlocks exploration. Review prayer material with a qualified teacher; references and caveats are in the learning guide. No depictions of Allah or prophets.

Tap **Qur’an audio → Start recitation** for optional recorded Juz Amma. The [community Internet Archive upload](https://archive.org/details/MuzammilHasballahJuzAmma) is labelled Muzammil Hasballah; it is not independently authenticated as an official release. No recording is redistributed in this repository. Streaming requires internet and may use substantial mobile data; Wi-Fi is recommended. Playback pauses for narration, activities, lessons, menus, breaks, hidden tabs, and grown-up notes. Individual ayah practice uses the existing Al-Husary recordings from EveryAyah.

### Privacy and compatibility

No accounts, ads, purchases, microphone, analytics, combat, enemies, or religious-performance scores. Discoveries, leaves, baby companion choice, completed hop-trail keepsake, position, and English voice preference stay in this browser’s local storage. No names or birthdays are stored. Third-party audio/font hosts receive ordinary network requests.

Use a recent Safari or Chrome with WebGL. If 3D is unavailable, use the illustrated adventure. Island graphics and scripts are bundled locally; no build step or runtime CDN is needed. No guaranteed offline installation is provided. Browser speech voices vary. Dinosaurs, fossils, and hatching eggs are stylised pretend play; the decorative pyramid is a labelled museum model, not a shared dinosaur/Egypt timeline.

Three.js is pinned to npm **0.160.1** under the included [MIT licence](vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt). The pinned classic build emits an upstream deprecation warning. All game models are procedurally constructed from shapes.
